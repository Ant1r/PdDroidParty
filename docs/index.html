Privacy Policy for PdDroidParty

Data Collection

PdDroidParty does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal or sensitive user data. All information or activity generated within the app stays locally on your device and is never sent to our servers or any third-party services.

Permissions

Our app only requests permissions that are strictly necessary for core functionality. We do not access your camera, contacts, location, or media files unless you explicitly trigger an in-app feature that requires it, and any data used remains on your device. The app accesses the microphone in order to provide real-time audio processing but does not send any recording from the microphone to a server.

Children’s Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any information from children.
