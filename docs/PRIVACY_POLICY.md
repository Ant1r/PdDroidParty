# Privacy Policy for PdDroidParty

- Effective Date: November 6, 2026
- Developer / Entity Name: McCormick IT Pty Ltd

## Data Collection
PdDroidParty does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal or sensitive user data. All information or activity generated within the app stays locally on your device and is never sent to our servers or any third-party services.

## Permissions
Our app only requests permissions that are strictly necessary for core functionality. We do not access your camera, contacts, location, or media files unless you explicitly trigger an in-app feature that requires it, and any data used remains on your device.
The app accesses the microphone in order to provide real-time audio processing but does not send any recording from the microphone to a server.

## Children’s Privacy
Our app does not knowingly collect any information from children.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
